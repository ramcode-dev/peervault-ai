# Writing Quality & Anti-Slop Directives

Preserve a natural, concrete human voice. Apply minimum effective edits. Never smooth into generic corporate prose. Use active voice and specific facts.

## Banned Patterns
- **Binary Contrasts**: Never use "It’s not X. It’s Y." State points directly.
- **Throat-Clearing Openers**: Cut "Here's the thing", "Let me be clear", "The uncomfortable truth is", "Here's what I mean".
- **Faux-Insight Setups**: Never use "What nobody tells you", "The part everyone misses", "This is the part most people skip".
- **Colon Reveals**: Never use a dramatic noun phrase followed by a colon and lowercase reveal ("The best part: it learns").
- **Trailing Superficial Analysis**: Cut trailing `-ing` clauses ("highlighting the commitment", "underscoring its significance").
- **Importance Puffery**: Cut "marks a pivotal moment", "stands as a testament to", "plays a vital role".
- **Interpretive Metadiscourse**: Cut asides telling readers how to feel ("That last part matters", "The key takeaway is").
- **Weasel Attribution**: Never use "experts agree", "studies show" without naming exact sources.
- **Dramatic Fragments & Clichés**: Cut "That’s it. That’s the whole thing.", "And that changes everything.", "Think about it:".
- **Banned Vocabulary**: *delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving*.
